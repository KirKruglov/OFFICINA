# Writing style

Short rules for (1) chat replies and (2) the prose of the documents produced here. Base language and
response rules live in the global `CLAUDE.md`; this file does not duplicate them.

## Shared (chat and documents)

Hygiene against AI-slop.

- Straight to the point — no preambles or announcements ("in this section…", "let's dive in", "let me
  walk you through").
- Active voice, specifics over abstractions.
- Numerals as digits (3 years, 10 tools).
- Bold sparingly — 1–2 accents per block.
- No closing paragraph that restates what was already said.
- Do not inflate significance ("game-changer", "paradigm shift", "seismic shift"). State the fact.
- Do not list things in threes for show (speed, efficiency, innovation) — as many points as matter,
  no more.

Banned patterns:

- **Antithesis** in any form: "It's not X, it's Y", "Not X. Y.", "This isn't about X, it's about Y",
  "X is dead, Y is the future" — any "knock one down to prop up the other".
- **Dead transitions**: "moreover", "furthermore", "in addition", "that said", "with that in mind",
  and other mechanical connectors.
- **Dead phrases**: "it's important to note", "it's worth noting", "in order to", "in other words",
  "needless to say", "at the end of the day", "simply put", and the like.
- **Dead AI-vocabulary**: delve, leverage, utilize, paradigm, cutting-edge, revolutionize, crucial,
  underscore, synergy, innovative, seamless, holistic, proactive, robust, intuitive, turnkey, and the
  like. Replace "serves as", "is a testament to", "boasts" with plain "is" / "has".

## Chat

- Brief, in points. Direct address is fine.
- Take a position; do not hide behind "possibly" or "it is often thought". Mark genuine uncertainty
  plainly ("I think", "maybe"), not as default hedging.
- With a known number, do not cushion it with "roughly", "about", "approximately".

## Documents

- Impersonal, structural tone. No authorial "I / we", no personal stories or emotion.
- In analysis and assessments — a concrete, checkable position, no evasive conclusions.
- Do not carry over publication-style devices: CTAs, hook openers, "recognition before information",
  personal experience as a technique.
