# docs/design

The product design system.

## Purpose
The design system and its rules.

- design-guide/ — components and design elements shared across the product
- template/     — reusable templates and standalone pages
- DESIGN.md     — description of the active design system and its rules

## Local rules
- One component or template — one file, kebab-case.
- Keep the system's rules in DESIGN.md; the folders hold materials under those rules.

## How to keep it
DESIGN.md is the living document of the system. A new component goes in design-guide/,
a finished page template in template/.
